PRISM is built for creators, media agencies, podcast networks, and video production teams who need to automate repetitive post-production tasks (reframing, cutting, dubbing, sound mastering) with frame accuracy.

PRISM connects directly through standard XML/EDL export formats, REST APIs, and native cloud render pipelines without altering your original camera raw assets.
